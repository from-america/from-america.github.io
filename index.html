§ From the founder

I'm Eric Spencer. FROM AMERICA LLC is my small studio — one person, no venture capital, no five-year roadmap. The work is iOS apps, web tools, and the occasional API. The goal is to ship a small number of careful things and stand behind them.

Each product is owned end-to-end: design, engineering, infrastructure, and support, by the same hands that conceived it. The throughline is the same across all of them — real problems, careful craft, and the patience to do a small number of things well.

If something here is useful to you, or you have a project worth making, I'd like to hear about it.

— Eric
