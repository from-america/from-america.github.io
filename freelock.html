Overview

FreeLock is an iOS app that uses physical NFC cards to restrict access to distracting applications. You scan an NFC-enabled card — a hotel key, transit card, or any tag — as a physical key, then lock selected apps until you tap that same card to your iPhone again.

The idea is simple: when your focus tool requires a physical object, the friction is real. No willpower hacks, no in-app loopholes — just a tap.

What it does

  • Block access to specified apps until an NFC card is tapped to the device
  • Quick setup using any NFC card you already own
  • Emergency unlock via biometrics with a mandatory delay
  • Optional accountability — notify trusted contacts when emergency unlocks occur
  • Battery-efficient background operation
  • Native iOS experience built with SwiftUI

Privacy

FreeLock stores all data locally on your device. There are no accounts, no tracking, and no cloud sync. The studio's stance is that a focus tool should never become the thing distracting you.
