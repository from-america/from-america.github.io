Overview

Stock Genie is a SwiftUI iPhone and iPad app that delivers a fresh set of AI-analyzed stock picks every trading day at 9:00 AM ET. A lightweight Cloudflare Worker backend pulls market data from Finnhub and Polygon and runs sentiment analysis through the HuggingFace Inference API.

Tiers

  • Free — the day's #1 pick, plus a 7-day history of past free picks
  • Premium ($4.99 / month) — all 5 daily picks and the full history view
  • One-off ($0.99) — unlock an extra pick without a subscription

How it's built

  • iOS app written in Swift and SwiftUI
  • Backend on Cloudflare Workers (Node.js, deployed via Wrangler)
  • Sentiment via @huggingface/inference; market data via Finnhub and Polygon
  • Subscriptions handled with RevenueCat and StoreKit 2
