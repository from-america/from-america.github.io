Stock Genie

A fresh batch of AI-curated stock picks every trading day at 9:00 AM Eastern. Quiet, opinionated, made for hobby traders.

Live Daily picks 9 AM ET Free & Premium

What it does

Every trading day, just before the bell, Stock Genie publishes a new set of picks with the reasoning shown up front — what's moving the stock, what the sentiment looks like, why it made the list. Open the app once, read for two minutes, get on with your day.

It is not a robo-adviser. It is not a trading platform. It is a quiet, opinionated daily read for people who enjoy the game.

Tiers

  • Free — the day's #1 pick, plus a 7-day history of past free picks
  • Premium · $4.99 / month — all 5 daily picks and the full history
  • One-off · $0.99 — unlock an extra pick without subscribing

What it isn't

  • Not financial advice — Stock Genie hands you opinions, not orders
  • Not a brokerage — picks happen in the app, trades happen elsewhere
  • Not noisy — one batch a day, no push at 3 a.m., no FOMO loops
