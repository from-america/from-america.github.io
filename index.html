§ From the founder

I'm Eric Spencer. FROM AMERICA LLC is my small studio — one person, no venture capital, no five-year roadmap. I make apps for iPhone and tools for the web. The goal is to ship a small number of careful things and stand behind them.

Each product is owned end-to-end by the same hands that conceived it. The throughline across all of them is the same — real problems, careful work, and the patience to do a small number of things well.

If something here is useful to you, or you have a project worth making, I'd like to hear about it.

— Eric
