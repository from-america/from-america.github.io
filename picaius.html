The editor, but on your phone

Picaius is what a real photo editor feels like when it shrinks to a handset. Layers stack. Brushes pressure-respond. Transforms snap. Undo goes back fifty steps. Open it once, work for an hour, never touch a sign-in screen.

What you can do

  • Build compositions in layers — stack, mask, blend
  • One-tap subject cut-outs that actually look clean
  • Perspective and warp transforms for posters, mockups, edits
  • Brush tools and text layers, with pressure where the device supports it
  • Up to fifty steps of undo / redo — really undo, not "remove last filter"
  • Save your work as a project and pick it back up tomorrow
  • Export the final to Photos at full quality

Why offline matters

Your photos stay on your device. Nothing uploads. Nothing trains on your work. The editor is just as fast on a plane as it is at your desk, and there is no quota waiting to throttle you on the day you finally have time to sit down with it.
